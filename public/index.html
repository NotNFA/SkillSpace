How SkillSpace works

Simple for clients. Valuable for creators. Built around direct connections.

01 / BUILD

Creator builds a profile

Add qualifications, experience, sample works, socials, availability and expected salary.

02 / HIRE

Client finds & hires

Open a creator's full profile, review everything and hit Hire Now when the match feels right.

03 / CONNECT

Both sides connect

The client gets a confirmation and the creator gets a notification. The creator can open the client's profile and connect through socials.

Terms & Conditions

Simple rules for a better SkillSpace.

These terms explain the basic expectations for using SkillSpace as a creator, editor, client or hirer.

By creating an account or using SkillSpace, you agree to use the platform responsibly and provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge. SkillSpace is a discovery and connection platform; project decisions and agreements remain between the people involved.

1. Account responsibility

Keep your login details secure and use only your own account. Do not impersonate another person or create misleading profiles.

2. Honest profiles & portfolios

Creators should share genuine skills, experience, qualifications and work samples. Do not upload work that you do not have permission to showcase.

3. Projects & communication

Clients and creators are responsible for discussing scope, pricing, deadlines, revisions and deliverables clearly before starting work.

4. Payments & contracts

Unless SkillSpace explicitly introduces an in-platform payment feature, payments, contracts and project terms are handled directly between the client and creator.

5. Community conduct

Do not use Community for spam, harassment, scams, illegal requests or misleading project listings. Keep conversations professional and relevant.

6. Platform use

Do not attempt to disrupt, abuse or gain unauthorized access to SkillSpace or its data. We may restrict accounts that seriously violate these rules.

Important: SkillSpace does not guarantee that every creator, client, project, portfolio, rating or claim on the platform is accurate or suitable. Users should review information carefully and make their own decisions before entering an agreement.
